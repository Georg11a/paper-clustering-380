Cluster membership is unchanged. Labels use deterministic phrase extraction, frequency–exclusivity reranking, abbreviation expansion, and lexical deduplication; no LLM is used.

## Noise / weak affinity (88 papers)

88 papers were left unassigned by the density model. They are retained as evidence of weak affinity rather than treated as an algorithm failure.

## Design Heuristics · Extraction, Formalization, and Evaluation (15 papers)

This 15-paper cluster focuses on the development and use of design heuristics as reusable guidance for ideation and evaluation. The papers emphasize how heuristics are extracted, classified, formalized, and adapted to domains such as products, dashboards, usability, and risk communication.

## HCI Design Knowledge · Models, Principles, Rules, and Heuristics (14 papers)

This 14-paper cluster focuses on the forms in which HCI design knowledge is represented and made actionable. It concentrates on models, methods, principles, rules, and engineering guidance for human–computer interaction rather than on a single application domain.

## Game Design Knowledge · Mechanics, Patterns, and Player Experience (21 papers)

This 21-paper cluster focuses on theories and reusable structures for designing games, gamification, and interactive narratives. Recurring concerns include game mechanics, design patterns, player experience, serious games, achievements, and design education.

## Visualization Design Knowledge · Visual Inquiry, Sonification, and Design Studies (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on knowledge and methods for designing visual and auditory representations of information. The cluster connects visualization design spaces and patterns with sonification, visual inquiry tools, design-study methodology, aesthetics, and narrative visualization.

## Design Methods · Description, Evaluation, and Ethical Practice (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on how design methods are described, assessed, corroborated, and used in practice. Unlike clusters centered on a substantive design domain, these papers examine method structure, effectiveness, rationale, situated use, and ethics-focused methods.

## Learning Design · Educational Patterns, Teacher Knowledge, and Blended Learning (18 papers)

This 18-paper cluster focuses on reusable design knowledge for teaching, learning environments, and technology-enhanced education. The papers address educational design patterns, teacher competencies, blended and online learning, learning analytics, scaffolding, and collaborative pattern-language development.

## Design Rationale · Argumentation, Documentation, and Reuse (22 papers)

This 22-paper cluster focuses on the capture and use of reasons, alternatives, and decisions produced during design. The cluster links argumentation structures with rationale documentation, design intent, communication, retrieval, and reuse; the abbreviation DR is normalized to design rationale.

## Design Principles · Formulation, Development, and Utilization (20 papers)

This 20-paper cluster focuses on the construction and practical use of design principles as prescriptive design knowledge. The papers examine principle anatomy, formulation, development from evidence, reporting quality, and transfer into organizational, visual, and educational settings.

## Formal Design Theories · C–K Theory, Mathematical Models, and Creative Preservation (11 papers)

This 11-paper cluster focuses on formal and mathematical accounts of design reasoning and innovation. C–K theory, general design theory, artifact and process models, and applications to creative preservation distinguish this cluster from broader discussions of theory construction.

## Human-Centered and Embodied Design · Agency, Empathy, and Reflective Practice (20 papers)

This 20-paper cluster focuses on human experience, embodiment, agency, and reflection in interaction and technology design. The papers range from design-oriented HCI and reflective practice to empathy, embodied co-design, posthuman critiques, sensory communication, and human-centeredness assessment.

## Public-Health and Participatory Design · Co-Design, Equity, and Health Systems (14 papers)

This 14-paper cluster focuses on participatory and value-sensitive design in public-health and health-related settings. The cluster emphasizes co-design, equitable access, health informatics, chronic-disease self-management, impact assessment, and the translation of design knowledge across disciplines.

## Interaction Patterns · Human–Robot, Human–AI, and Pervasive Systems (9 papers)

This 9-paper cluster focuses on reusable interaction patterns and design spaces for intelligent and pervasive systems. Human–robot sociality, human–AI interaction primitives, pervasive interaction, and system interaction patterns provide the cluster's shared context.

## Engineering Product Design · Complex Products, Manufacturing, and Prototyping (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on systematic methods and theories for engineering products and production-oriented innovation. The papers cover complex and mechanical products, additive manufacturing, hybrid components and prototyping, robust design, proof of concept, and product recovery.

## Information-Systems Design Theory · Digital, Community, and Socio-Technical Systems (16 papers)

This 16-paper cluster focuses on the construction and application of design theories for information systems. The cluster includes web-based education, communities, platforms, information infrastructures, privacy, security laboratories, integration management, and explainable systems.

## Design Theory Construction · Criteria, Epistemology, and Research through Design (23 papers)

This 23-paper cluster focuses on how design theories and knowledge claims are constructed, evaluated, and justified. It foregrounds theory criteria, epistemology, definitions, research through design, designerly research methods, and relations between theory, artifacts, and practice.

## Design Science Research Knowledge · Codification, Accumulation, and Reuse (25 papers)

This 25-paper cluster focuses on the production and management of design knowledge contributions in design science research. The papers emphasize knowledge typologies, contribution reporting, codification, accumulation mechanisms, reusable principles, ontologies, problem and solution spaces, and meta-studies.

## Forms of Design Knowledge · Product, Industrial, and Tacit Knowledge (19 papers)

This 19-paper cluster focuses on the forms, levels, representations, and transfer of design knowledge. Industrial and product design, tacit and intermediate knowledge, prototyping, education, cognition, and knowledge representation anchor this cluster.

## Designer Expertise · Practice Knowledge, Philosophy, and Communities (8 papers)

This 8-paper cluster focuses on what designers know and how expertise is formed, researched, and legitimated. The cluster brings together design expertise, sociology and philosophy of design knowledge, practice-based research, instructional-design expertise, and practitioner communities.

## Organizational and AI-Enabled Systems · Modularity, Data, and Design Rules (33 papers)

This 33-paper cluster focuses on frameworks and design rules for organizational, enterprise, data-intensive, and AI-enabled systems. Modularity, platforms, business and software-process design, data ecosystems, AI governance, cognitive engineering, system integration, and organizational behavior make this a broad systems-oriented cluster.

## Software Architecture and Interfaces · Design Spaces, Rules, and Abstraction (23 papers)

This 23-paper cluster focuses on architectural knowledge for designing software systems and user interfaces. The shared emphasis is on design spaces, architectural rules and styles, abstraction, interface structure, implementation guidance, and ecological interface foundations.

## Emerging Software and AI Patterns · Languages, Agents, and Domain Applications (21 papers)

This 21-paper cluster focuses on pattern-based guidance for emerging software, AI, and domain-specific systems. Compared with the other pattern clusters, this group is more heterogeneous and application-led, spanning AI agents, domain-specific languages, retrieval-augmented generation, health algorithms, biological systems, and fluent interfaces.

## Architectural Patterns · Reference Architectures, Services, and Components (8 papers)

This 8-paper cluster focuses on patterns for organizing software architectures and reusable system structures. Reference architectures, layers and styles, component-oriented development, service engineering, Tropos, and blockchain interoperability distinguish it from code-level and interaction-oriented pattern clusters.

## Software Design Patterns · Object-Oriented Reuse, Composition, and Formalization (32 papers)

This 32-paper cluster focuses on the representation and application of reusable design solutions in software engineering. Object-oriented architecture, composition, formal semantics, refactoring, security taxonomies, and industrial pattern use distinguish this cluster from pattern languages centered on human practice.

## Pattern Languages · Interaction, Learning, and Collaborative Practice (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on pattern languages as mechanisms for capturing and communicating reusable design knowledge. Unlike the software-centered pattern cluster, these papers apply patterns to interaction design, learning, project communication, participatory futures, collaborative knowledge, and service co-creation.

Cluster membership is unchanged. Labels use deterministic phrase extraction, frequency–exclusivity reranking, abbreviation expansion, and lexical deduplication; no LLM is used.

## Noise / weak affinity (88 papers)

88 papers were left unassigned by the density model. They are retained as evidence of weak affinity rather than treated as an algorithm failure.

## Design Heuristics · Extraction, Formalization, and Evaluation (15 papers)

This 15-paper cluster focuses on the development and use of design heuristics as reusable guidance for ideation and evaluation. The papers emphasize how heuristics are extracted, classified, formalized, and adapted to domains such as products, dashboards, usability, and risk communication. Representative papers include Design heuristics: A conceptual framework and preliminary method for extraction; Design Heuristics in Innovative Products; Formal system for the expression of target-oriented design heuristics.

## HCI Design Knowledge · Models, Principles, Rules, and Heuristics (14 papers)

This 14-paper cluster focuses on the forms in which HCI design knowledge is represented and made actionable. It concentrates on models, methods, principles, rules, and engineering guidance for human–computer interaction rather than on a single application domain. Representative papers include HCI Design Knowledge; HCI Knowledge; HCI Design Knowledge as Models and Methods.

## Game Design Knowledge · Mechanics, Patterns, and Player Experience (21 papers)

This 21-paper cluster focuses on theories and reusable structures for designing games, gamification, and interactive narratives. Recurring concerns include game mechanics, design patterns, player experience, serious games, achievements, and design education. Representative papers include A Theoretical Model for Game Mechanics: Bridging Design Practice and Education; Game Design as an Autonomous Research Subject; Form and Function: Toward a Better Understanding of Design-Based Games.

## Visualization Design Knowledge · Visual Inquiry, Sonification, and Design Studies (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on knowledge and methods for designing visual and auditory representations of information. The cluster connects visualization design spaces and patterns with sonification, visual inquiry tools, design-study methodology, aesthetics, and narrative visualization. Representative papers include Revisiting the Design Patterns of Composite Visualizations; Exploring the design space of composite visualization; Structured Visualization Design Knowledge for Grounding Generative Reasoning and Situated Feedback.

## Design Methods · Description, Evaluation, and Ethical Practice (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on how design methods are described, assessed, corroborated, and used in practice. Unlike clusters centered on a substantive design domain, these papers examine method structure, effectiveness, rationale, situated use, and ethics-focused methods. Representative papers include Elements of a design method - a basis for describing and evaluating design methods; Elements of a design method – a basis for describing and evaluating design methods; Evaluating the efficacy and effectiveness of design methods: A systematic review and assessment framework.

## Learning Design · Educational Patterns, Teacher Knowledge, and Blended Learning (18 papers)

This 18-paper cluster focuses on reusable design knowledge for teaching, learning environments, and technology-enhanced education. The papers address educational design patterns, teacher competencies, blended and online learning, learning analytics, scaffolding, and collaborative pattern-language development. Representative papers include The Value of Design Patterns in Designing Teaching in Online Settings; Developing a competency taxonomy for teacher design knowledge in technology-enhanced learning environments: a literature review; Grounded design: design patterns as the link between theory and practice.

## Design Rationale · Argumentation, Documentation, and Reuse (22 papers)

This 22-paper cluster focuses on the capture and use of reasons, alternatives, and decisions produced during design. The cluster links argumentation structures with rationale documentation, design intent, communication, retrieval, and reuse; the abbreviation DR is normalized to design rationale. Representative papers include Integrating different perspectives on design rationale: supporting the emergence of design rationale from design communication; Redesigning the rationale for design rationale; Integrating different perspective on design rationale: Supporting the emergence of design rationale from design communication.

## Design Principles · Formulation, Development, and Utilization (20 papers)

This 20-paper cluster focuses on the construction and practical use of design principles as prescriptive design knowledge. The papers examine principle anatomy, formulation, development from evidence, reporting quality, and transfer into organizational, visual, and educational settings. Representative papers include Towards a Method for Design Principle Development in Information Systems; Design Principles: Literature Review, Analysis, and Future Directions; Guidelines supporting the formulation of design principles.

## Formal Design Theories · C–K Theory, Mathematical Models, and Creative Preservation (11 papers)

This 11-paper cluster focuses on formal and mathematical accounts of design reasoning and innovation. C–K theory, general design theory, artifact and process models, and applications to creative preservation distinguish this cluster from broader discussions of theory construction. Representative papers include C-K design theory: an advanced formulation; A new approach of innovative design: An introduction to C-K theory; A mathematical theory of design: representation of design artifacts (Part I).

## Human-Centered and Embodied Design · Agency, Empathy, and Reflective Practice (20 papers)

This 20-paper cluster focuses on human experience, embodiment, agency, and reflection in interaction and technology design. The papers range from design-oriented HCI and reflective practice to empathy, embodied co-design, posthuman critiques, sensory communication, and human-centeredness assessment. Representative papers include Design-oriented human-computer interaction; Designing as reflective conversation with the materials of a design situation; A theoretical framework on embodiment in digital design.

## Public-Health and Participatory Design · Co-Design, Equity, and Health Systems (14 papers)

This 14-paper cluster focuses on participatory and value-sensitive design in public-health and health-related settings. The cluster emphasizes co-design, equitable access, health informatics, chronic-disease self-management, impact assessment, and the translation of design knowledge across disciplines. Representative papers include Cutting Cubes Out of Fog: The Whole View of Design; Design epistemology as innovation in public health research; Universal Participatory Design: Achievements and Challenges.

## Interaction Patterns · Human–Robot, Human–AI, and Pervasive Systems (9 papers)

This 9-paper cluster focuses on reusable interaction patterns and design spaces for intelligent and pervasive systems. Human–robot sociality, human–AI interaction primitives, pervasive interaction, and system interaction patterns provide the cluster's shared context. Representative papers include Design patterns for exploring and prototyping human-robot interactions; Design patterns for sociality in human-robot interaction; An overview of human-computer interaction patterns in pervasive systems.

## Engineering Product Design · Complex Products, Manufacturing, and Prototyping (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on systematic methods and theories for engineering products and production-oriented innovation. The papers cover complex and mechanical products, additive manufacturing, hybrid components and prototyping, robust design, proof of concept, and product recovery. Representative papers include Design theory: Methods and organization for innovation; Design Theory and Method of Complex Products: A Review; The Conceptual Design of Mechanical Products.

## Information-Systems Design Theory · Digital, Community, and Socio-Technical Systems (16 papers)

This 16-paper cluster focuses on the construction and application of design theories for information systems. The cluster includes web-based education, communities, platforms, information infrastructures, privacy, security laboratories, integration management, and explainable systems. Representative papers include Web-based information systems: Developing a design theory; Design Theory in Information Systems; Building an information system design theory for vigilant EIS.

## Design Theory Construction · Criteria, Epistemology, and Research through Design (23 papers)

This 23-paper cluster focuses on how design theories and knowledge claims are constructed, evaluated, and justified. It foregrounds theory criteria, epistemology, definitions, research through design, designerly research methods, and relations between theory, artifacts, and practice. Representative papers include What should we expect from research through design?; Four Criteria for Design Theories; Theory construction in design research. Criteria, approaches, and methods..

## Design Science Research Knowledge · Codification, Accumulation, and Reuse (25 papers)

This 25-paper cluster focuses on the production and management of design knowledge contributions in design science research. The papers emphasize knowledge typologies, contribution reporting, codification, accumulation mechanisms, reusable principles, ontologies, problem and solution spaces, and meta-studies. Representative papers include A Typology of Knowledge Creation in Design Science Research Projects; Utilizing, Producing, and Contributing Design Knowledge in DSR Projects; Design Science Research: Guidance for Crafting and Reporting Design Knowledge Contributions.

## Forms of Design Knowledge · Product, Industrial, and Tacit Knowledge (19 papers)

This 19-paper cluster focuses on the forms, levels, representations, and transfer of design knowledge. Industrial and product design, tacit and intermediate knowledge, prototyping, education, cognition, and knowledge representation anchor this cluster. Representative papers include Toward a Unified Model of Design Knowledge; A typology of design knowledge: A theoretical framework; Investigations into the data basis of design knowledge in industrial design engineering.

## Designer Expertise · Practice Knowledge, Philosophy, and Communities (8 papers)

This 8-paper cluster focuses on what designers know and how expertise is formed, researched, and legitimated. The cluster brings together design expertise, sociology and philosophy of design knowledge, practice-based research, instructional-design expertise, and practitioner communities. Representative papers include What designers know; Legitimating design: a sociology of knowledge account of the field; Towards an integrative approach to researching design expertise.

## Organizational and AI-Enabled Systems · Modularity, Data, and Design Rules (33 papers)

This 33-paper cluster focuses on frameworks and design rules for organizational, enterprise, data-intensive, and AI-enabled systems. Modularity, platforms, business and software-process design, data ecosystems, AI governance, cognitive engineering, system integration, and organizational behavior make this a broad systems-oriented cluster. Representative papers include A conceptual framework for design science research; Towards normal design for safety-critical systems; Management design theories.

## Software Architecture and Interfaces · Design Spaces, Rules, and Abstraction (23 papers)

This 23-paper cluster focuses on architectural knowledge for designing software systems and user interfaces. The shared emphasis is on design spaces, architectural rules and styles, abstraction, interface structure, implementation guidance, and ecological interface foundations. Representative papers include Studying Software Architecture Through Design Spaces and Rules; Design Space and Design Rules for User Interface Software Architecture; Ecological interface design: theoretical foundations.

## Emerging Software and AI Patterns · Languages, Agents, and Domain Applications (21 papers)

This 21-paper cluster focuses on pattern-based guidance for emerging software, AI, and domain-specific systems. Compared with the other pattern clusters, this group is more heterogeneous and application-led, spanning AI agents, domain-specific languages, retrieval-augmented generation, health algorithms, biological systems, and fluent interfaces. Representative papers include Analysis patterns; Typed Design Patterns for the Functional Era; Principles versus patterns.

## Architectural Patterns · Reference Architectures, Services, and Components (8 papers)

This 8-paper cluster focuses on patterns for organizing software architectures and reusable system structures. Reference architectures, layers and styles, component-oriented development, service engineering, Tropos, and blockchain interoperability distinguish it from code-level and interaction-oriented pattern clusters. Representative papers include Conceptual patterns; Architecture pattern organization; Layers, decisions, patterns, styles, and architectures.

## Software Design Patterns · Object-Oriented Reuse, Composition, and Formalization (32 papers)

This 32-paper cluster focuses on the representation and application of reusable design solutions in software engineering. Object-oriented architecture, composition, formal semantics, refactoring, security taxonomies, and industrial pattern use distinguish this cluster from pattern languages centered on human practice. Representative papers include Industrial experience with design patterns; Understanding and using patterns in software development; “Modeling-by-Patterns” of Web applications.

## Pattern Languages · Interaction, Learning, and Collaborative Practice (12 papers)

This 12-paper cluster focuses on pattern languages as mechanisms for capturing and communicating reusable design knowledge. Unlike the software-centered pattern cluster, these papers apply patterns to interaction design, learning, project communication, participatory futures, collaborative knowledge, and service co-creation. Representative papers include A pattern approach to interaction design; A pattern language for designing innovative projects: project design patterns; Pattern language: A framework for learning.
